# Rinko — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Rinko collects, why, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Rinko app and API, operated by **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]** ("Rinko," "we," "us").

Rinko exists to give **you** independence over your own work record. That principle shapes this policy: we collect only what the app needs to build your record, we do not sell your data, and we do not share your work data with carriers or anyone else without your action (e.g., you choosing to export and send it yourself).

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information**
- Name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).

**Work record data you create**
- Carriers you log (e.g., "UniUni"), work sessions (route ID, service date, start/end time, packages assigned/completed, exceptions, mileage, agreed rate), payment records (expected gross, amount received, dates), and any notes you add.

**Evidence you upload**
- Screenshots, photos, and PDF documents you attach as evidence (e.g., route screenshots, rate screenshots, GPS session exports, settlement statements). These files may incidentally contain personal information about you or third parties (for example, an address visible in a screenshot) — you control what you upload.

**Automatically collected data**
- Basic technical data needed to operate the service and keep it secure: IP address, timestamps, and request logs. Rinko does not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs that profile you across other apps or sites.

We do **not** collect real-time GPS location from your device. If a "GPS session" is part of your evidence, it's a file you chose to export from another app and upload — Rinko does not track your location itself.

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information only to:
- create and secure your account (authentication);
- store and display your work sessions, evidence, Work Reports, and Payment Ledger;
- perform the calculations you use the app for (expected gross pay, payment difference, outstanding balance);
- let you export your data (CSV, JSON, `.docx`);
- provide customer support when you contact us;
- maintain, secure, and improve the service (e.g., debugging, abuse prevention);
- comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your work data to train third-party advertising models, and we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to providing you the service, without asking you first.
